About this prototype

See the person, not the screen.

HoloCall turns an ordinary phone camera into an augmented view. No headset, no app install, no special camera.

How it works

  1. Cut out. An AI model on the viewer's device separates the person (or the product you tap) from the background, 30 times a second.
  2. Add depth. The cut-out is bent forward using its own shape, so it has volume when you look around it.
  3. Place it. Show it in a virtual room, over your camera, life-size on your real floor (WebXR), or in side-by-side 3D for glasses.
  4. Send it. Video goes straight between devices (WebRTC). The server only introduces them.

Works today

  • Any modern phone or laptop browser. "Place in room" needs Chrome on an ARCore Android phone.
  • Cardboard viewers and display glasses (Xreal, Rokid) through Glasses 3D.
  • Calls between two people; Live, Class and Stage up to 12 viewers each.

Limits

  • 2.5D, not full 3D: one camera can't see someone's back, so turning is limited to about 60°.
  • Cut-outs work best with one person, upper body in frame, and good light.

Going big

  • Thousands of viewers: swap direct connections for a media server (LiveKit or Cloudflare Calls), so the presenter uploads once.
  • Reliable connections: run our own signalling and TURN relay instead of the free public ones.
  • True 3D: two or more phones around the presenter, or a depth camera, fused into a volumetric model.
